How WoollyDesk works

Getting started

WoollyDesk is a menu bar app for macOS 14 Sonoma or later. It has no Dock icon and no big window. The farm lives on your desktop wallpaper, behind all your app windows. Close or move a window to see your sheep.

  1. Open WoollyDesk. A small cloud-and-sun icon appears in the menu bar at the top of the screen.
  2. Your farm appears on the desktop with a few sheep.
  3. Click a sheep to meet it. Click it again to pet it.
Do not worry about a busy week. Nothing bad happens while your Mac is closed or asleep.

The menu bar

Click the cloud-and-sun icon in the menu bar. The menu is the main control panel of the farm. From it you can:

  • open the Farm, the Shop and Trade, or start to Build;
  • see your flock and the sheep that need attention;
  • choose the weather, the season and the time of day, or let the farm follow the real ones;
  • put the sheep above or below your desktop icons;
  • pause the farm: it then uses no CPU;
  • open the Farmer's guide;
  • open the Settings, or quit the app.

On the desktop

The two round buttons

Two round buttons sit at the edge of the screen: Home and Tools. Click a circle and its buttons slide out. Click anywhere else and they slide back in. Drag a circle to any edge of the screen, and it stays there. You can hide the buttons in the settings.

Clicking things

  • Click a sheep, a building or a gate to see what it can do.
  • All other clicks go through to Finder, so your files and icons work as usual.
  • Want a calm wallpaper only? Turn off Sheep above desktop icons in the menu.
Windows move aside when you click the grass? This is a macOS feature: a click on the wallpaper shows the desktop. To turn it off, open System Settings → Desktop & Dock and set Click wallpaper to reveal desktop to Only in Stage Manager. Clicks on sheep, buildings and the round buttons always work. WoollyDesk also shows this tip in Settings → Desktop.

Settings

Open the menu and choose Settings…, or use the Home circle on the desktop.

Language

English or Ukrainian, or the language of your Mac.

Weather and time

Real weather for your area, or a weather, season and time of day that you choose.

Desktop

The round buttons, sheep names and mood bubbles, autumn leaves on your icons, farm-style folder icons.

Farm life

How fast the sheep grow up, lambs, and the size of the flock.

Sound

Soft rain, wind and bleats, tool sounds, and the volume. Sound is off by default.

Farm

Start a new farm when you want a fresh beginning.

Real weather comes from Apple. WoollyDesk gets the weather from Apple WeatherKit, the service behind the Weather app on your Mac. To show the weather where you live, the app needs your approximate location, so macOS asks for your permission once. The app rounds the location to about 10 km and does not save it. Do not want to share your location? Turn on Real weather for my area, type any city in City (optional) and click Apply. The farm then shows the weather in that city.

The full farmer's guide tells you everything about sheep care, wool, the shop, buildings, seasons and holidays. It comes with the game.

Already have WoollyDesk? Click the cloud-and-sun icon in the menu bar and choose Farmer's guide.
