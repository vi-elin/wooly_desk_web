Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

No account

You never sign up or log in.

No ads

No ads and no tracking across apps or websites. Anonymous usage data is optional.

No cloud

Your farm is saved only on your Mac.

This policy explains what the WoollyDesk app for macOS (the "app") and this website do with information. The app is made by an independent developer. Contact: vielindevelopment@gmail.com.

1. Information the app keeps on your Mac

The app saves your farm (sheep, names, buildings, coins, wool) and your settings on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/SheepFarm/ and in the app's standard preferences. This information never leaves your Mac. We cannot see it. You can delete it at any time: delete that folder, or use click Start over… in Settings → Farm.

2. Real weather and location (optional)

Real weather is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings → Weather and time:

  • You can type a city. Apple's geocoding service in macOS turns the city name into map coordinates.
  • Or you can leave the city empty. Then macOS asks for your permission to use Location Services, and the app uses your approximate location.
  • The app rounds the coordinates to about 10 km and sends only these rounded numbers to Apple WeatherKit to get the current weather. The app uses no other weather service. No name, email or other identifier goes with the request.
  • The app keeps the last weather on your Mac, so the farm works offline.

Apple receives these requests like any internet request, including your IP address. Apple's privacy policy applies. You can turn real weather off at any time, and you can remove the location permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

3. Finder permission (optional)

If you allow autumn leaves on your desktop icons, macOS asks once if the app may control Finder. The app asks Finder only for the positions of the icons on your desktop. It does not read, change or send your files. You can turn this feature off in Settings → Desktop, and remove the permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation.

4. Anonymous usage data and crash reports (optional)

To find bugs and see which parts of the game people use, the app sends anonymous usage data to Google Firebase (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics). The switch Share anonymous usage data in Settings → Privacy turns this on or off. It is on by default.

  • What is sent: crash reports, and simple counts such as which windows and tabs you open, which desktop buttons you use, which settings you change and which shop items you get. Firebase also receives the app version, the macOS version, the Mac model, a random ID for this copy of the app and, like any internet request, your IP address.
  • What is never sent: your name, email, sheep names, the city you type, your location, or the names of your files and folders.
  • We use this data only to fix problems and improve the game. We do not use it for ads and we do not sell it.

Turn off Share anonymous usage data at any time, and the app stops sending. Google handles this data under its own policies: Firebase privacy and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not sell your information to anyone.
  • It does not record your screen, your keystrokes or what you do in other apps. It only sees where your mouse pointer is, so sheep can react to it.
  • It has no in-app purchases.

6. When you write to support

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you and to fix problems. We do not add you to a mailing list. Ask us at any time and we delete your messages.

7. The Windows waitlist (optional)

If you leave your email in the "WoollyDesk for Windows" window, we keep it in a private Notion database, together with the page language and the button you used. A small Cloudflare Worker passes it from this website to Notion. We use your email only to send you one message when the Windows version is out. We do not share or sell it, and we do not add it to any other list. Write to vielindevelopment@gmail.com and we delete it. We also delete the whole list after the announcement. Notion and Cloudflare handle this data under their own privacy policies: Notion, Cloudflare.

8. This website

  • The website uses no cookies.
  • The website counts visits with Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It counts which pages people open, where they come from (for example, a search engine), how far they scroll and how many people click “Get WoollyDesk” or ask for a Windows version. It stores no cookies and collects no personal information. It does not follow you to other websites, and nobody can identify you from it. See the Umami privacy policy.
  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • The fonts come from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to load them. See the Google Privacy Policy.

9. Children

WoollyDesk is suitable for all ages. The app does not ask anyone for personal information, including children. The optional usage data has no names or contact details.

10. Your rights

The app sends no name or contact details, so we cannot link its usage data to you. To stop it, turn off Share anonymous usage data. For emails you sent us, or your address on the Windows waitlist, write to vielindevelopment@gmail.com to see or delete them.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. Important changes are also mentioned in the app's release notes.
