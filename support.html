Help us help you

If something does not work, please add these details to your email. They help us find the problem much faster:

Your Mac

The macOS version (Apple menu → About This Mac) and the number of displays.

The app version

In Finder, select WoollyDesk in Applications and press ⌘I.

What happened

What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

A screenshot

Press ⇧⌘4 and select the area. A picture of the farm often explains everything.

Quick fixes

I cannot see the farm

The farm is behind your windows. Hide them with ⌘⌥H or move them away. If the farm is still not there, check that the farm is not paused in the menu.

I cannot click my sheep

Turn on Sheep above desktop icons in the menu or in Settings → Desktop.

The weather on the farm is wrong

Open Settings → Weather and time. Check that Real weather for my area is on and that the line under the city field shows your place. You can also type your city and click Apply.

The round buttons are in my way

Drag a circle to another edge of the screen, or turn the buttons off in Settings → Desktop.
